## ninja

Arquivo: public/classes/ninja-v2.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: single full-body transparent 2D cutout for a funny tabletop RPG card. Input image: strict STYLE reference only. Match its crude charming funny doodle look very closely: uneven hand-drawn dark brown ink lines with visibly wobbly strokes, very simple angular shapes, tiny limbs, big awkward heads, wonky faces, sparse flat muted solid colors. Must look like a quick funny pen cartoon, NOT polished digital concept art. NO bevels, NO lighting, NO gradients, NO paper texture, NO painterly shading, NO realistic anatomy, NO ornate armor, NO detailed fabric. Almost completely flat. Intentional awkward proportions and slapstick pose. Subject: Ninja do Visto por Último. A scrawny turquoise cyclops goblin in a floppy midnight blue hood. One gigantic tired eyeball, ridiculously long nose, tiny twig legs and enormous flat slippers. He hides a tiny scroll behind his back while pretending to be invisible. Scarf sticking out like a crooked flag. A few little flies of surprise. Only 5 flat colors. One complete character centered alone with transparent padding, full feet and props visible; for Domador include his dog as part of same duo. No scenic background, no ground plane, no text, no labels, no border, no card frame. Actual transparent alpha background. Do not copy exact characters from reference, make a new original character with the same simple humorous drawing language.
